Skee-Ball Machine

Along with 2 other people, I planned, budgeted, and constructed a Skee-Ball machine. The machine is complete with a mounted monitor and a real-time scoring system managed by a Raspberry Pi, integrating physical sensors with a python backend.

As the lead of everything electrical and programming, I implemented a state machine with updates for score tracking, designed logic to allow tolerance for missed or duplicate sensor signals, and integrated hardware inputs with the game logic.

Holy Ship — Published on Steam

On a team of 2 people, Holy Ship was developed using the Unity game engine with C#. I developed many of the core gameplay systems, scene management, UI, and architecture. I worked with the team member to manage the Steam publishing pipeline and iterated based on playtesting and user feedback.

After completing a postmortum of the project and discussing places we went right and wrong, we are currently working on a complete refactor/rebuild of the game. Feel free to ask me about this process!
